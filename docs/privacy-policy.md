# Privacy Policy for "Leg & Lær"

*Last updated: 20 July 2026*

## In short: we collect no data

"Leg & Lær" ("Play & Learn") is made for children and is private by design.
The app:

- **collects no personal data** of any kind,
- **sends no data** to us or to any third party,
- **has no user accounts, logins or profiles,**
- **shows no ads** and contains no advertising or analytics SDKs,
- **requests no device permissions,**
- **works fully offline** – there are no network calls in the children's part.

## What is stored – and where?

The only thing the app stores is **game progress** (earned stars, difficulty
level and the sound setting). This information:

- is stored **solely on the device** (the browser's `localStorage`),
- **never** leaves the device,
- can be deleted at any time in the app under *Parents & settings → Reset all
  stars*, or by uninstalling the app.

It is not linked to any name, e-mail, device ID or location.

## Children

Because the app collects and shares no data, it complies with Google Play
Families policies, GDPR and COPPA by design. There is no chat, no social
features and no links out of the app outside the parents' section.

## Audio and graphics

All speech is pre-recorded and bundled in the app (Danish neural TTS). All
graphics are drawn and bundled locally. Nothing is fetched from the internet
during use.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here and in the app's
parents' section.
